Join IMSL

We are recruiting graduate students (M.S., Ph.D., integrated M.S.-Ph.D.) and undergraduate research interns.

Students interested in thermal-fluid engineering, CFD, hydrogen and fuel cells, or AI are welcome regardless of their background.

  • Multiphysics CFD with Ansys Fluent and programming in Python/MATLAB
  • Design projects on real energy devices such as SOFC/SOEC and batteries
  • Joint research with institutes and companies in Korea and abroad, and presentations at international conferences

Career Pathways

We support career paths through our industry-academia network, including government research institutes such as KIMM, HD Group, and Samsung Group.

How to apply

Send your CV and a short statement of your research interests to ygbae@dgu.ac.kr.
