About IMSL

Understand the physics, model it quantitatively, and turn it into design

Energy conversion devices couple reaction, heat, flow, and electricity at the same time. Looking at the reaction alone or the flow alone cannot predict how a real device performs. IMSL resolves these phenomena together with multiphysics CFD and combines it with AI to design next-generation hydrogen and zero-carbon power devices such as SOFC/SOEC stacks.

Research

Research Areas

On a consistent CFD methodology, we have steadily expanded the physics we resolve: from fluid dynamics to surface reactions, electrochemistry, and AI-integrated design.

01

Multiphysics CFD for SOFC/SOEC

3D multiphysics analysis of solid oxide fuel and electrolysis cells
  • High-fidelity 3D models coupling mass, momentum, energy, charge, and reaction (Ansys Fluent + UDF)
  • From a single channel (1 cm x 10 cm) to commercial cells (15 x 15 cm2) and stacks (15 x 15 x 25 cm3)
  • Transient thermo-electrochemical behavior under load change
  • Bipolar plate and manifold design by controlling thermal and flow resistance
Temperature deviation -65%Flow deviation -60%Stack ΔT 66 -> 45 °C
02

Reacting Flow & Reaction Kinetics

Surface reacting-flow analysis and reaction mechanism development
  • 2D flow + surface reaction models of catalytic CH4 reforming reactors
  • Automated optimization of surface reaction mechanisms with a genetic algorithm (GA)
  • Reaction pathways identified with in-situ XPS, FT-IR, and DFT calculations
  • Experimental validation with GC/MS residual gas analysis (RGA)
Micro-kineticsGenetic algorithmCatalytic reactor
03

AI-CFD Integrated Design

Multi-objective optimal design combining AI and CFD
  • Bayesian optimization and active learning to cut CFD computing cost
  • Gaussian process (GP) multi-objective surrogate models and ε-PAL Pareto search
  • SOEC operating conditions that lower the thermal gradient (ΔT) and raise efficiency (η)
  • Stack design platform based on physics-informed neural networks (PINN)
Bayesian optimizationSurrogate modelPINN

Stack & System for Green H2

NH3-SOFC and SOEC stack fabrication and system analysis
  • In-house 1 kW-class NH3-SOFC full stack (40 cells) with long-term operation
  • Pressurized (3 bar) NH3-SOFC system co-producing electricity and hydrogen
  • Internal cascade stacks that maximize fuel utilization
  • Next-generation SOEC with steam sweep gas on the air electrode

Highlights

Research Highlights

"CFD is a tool for solving real problems." Our work goes beyond analysis to design, fabrication, validation, and technology transfer.

NH3-SOFC stack design optimization

A dual alternative cross-flow design cut the temperature difference inside the stack from 66 °C to 45 °C (-31.8%). (Energy Conversion and Management, 2023)

Improved stacking technology

Better bipolar plate roughness, sealing, and an alignment guide reduced the cell-to-cell voltage deviation of a 5-cell stack from 0.36 V to 0.045 V and raised power by 23.3%.

Pressurized NH3-SOFC for electricity and hydrogen

Operated in a 3 bar pressurized chamber, the system co-produces electricity and high-purity hydrogen with a total efficiency of 88.1%.

AI-CFD optimization of SOEC operation

CFD results are added to a GP surrogate through active learning to find the Pareto optimum between ΔT and efficiency. (Applied Thermal Engineering, 2026)
